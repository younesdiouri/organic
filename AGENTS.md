## MVP scope
French mobile-friendly admin app for one restaurant. Login, clients, product catalogue, deliveries with quantities and unit prices snapshotted, occasional dated returns attached to delivery lines, per-client date-range recap, payments and CSV export. Currency MAD, store amounts as integer centimes, never float arithmetic for stored money. Default product prices with editable delivery prices suffice; no contract pricing engine.
A delivery is recorded on its day; return later preserves original delivery. Sum of returns cannot exceed delivered quantity; reject negative/zero quantities, invalid dates and invalid money. Guard concurrent return submissions transactionally. Payments are dated entries per client; do not infer paid status from an arbitrary reporting window. Reports distinguish period activity and cumulative balance as of end date. CSV must resist spreadsheet formula injection.
No client portal, orders, lots, QR, global inventory, automated Sheets sync, invoice/tax engine. Fly deployment is explicitly authorized, subject to the local review requirement above; do not deploy or change other projects. Demo records must be clearly fictitious. The user authorized importing the supplied delivery catalogue and technical recipe workbooks; keep source files, extracts, private supplier registries and manifests outside Git and Docker build layers. Protect all business routes, CSRF on mutations, proper password hashing. Avoid destructive editing of delivery/payment history.

## Catalogue and recipes
Support editable ingredients, nested preparations, dishes and packaging in one coherent article catalogue, with delivery availability, purchase prices/pack sizes/yields and existing supplier links. Les Maîtres des Délices is the canonical supplier; LES MAITRES DU PAIN is its previous name/alias. ORGANIC KITCHEN is the restaurant itself, never a supplier. WRAP TUNA/WRAP TOUNA, WRAP BEIJING/WRAP PEKINOISE and HOME LIMONADE/OK'S HOMEMADE LEMONADE are confirmed equivalent names. Smoothies have multiple flavours; do not infer their delivery formats or map the generic 45 MAD tariff to every 48 cl recipe. Preserve uncertain source values visibly in editable draft recipes rather than inventing quantities, yields, prices or packaging assignments. Distinguish batch preparation from per-portion assembly. Imports must be previewable, transactional, repeatable without duplicates and preserve manual edits. Monetary amounts remain integer centimes; nested technical calculations must use exact decimal arithmetic without binary floats or premature cent rounding.
